Sacred Steps

The 18 Sacred Steps (Pathinettampadi) Thathvam

Each of the 18 steps leading to the sanctum sanctorum represents the progressive conquest of human frailties and the realization of Tat Tvam Asi (That Thou Art).

1
Indriya Nigraham (Senses)

1. Kan / Sight

Purifying visual perception; seeing divinity in all creations.

2
Indriya Nigraham (Senses)

2. Sevi / Hearing

Listening solely to noble words, chants, and righteous discourse.

3
Indriya Nigraham (Senses)

3. Mookku / Smell

Breathing pure life force; detachment from transient material desires.

4
Indriya Nigraham (Senses)

4. Naakku / Taste

Restraint in speech and consumption of sattvic vegetarian nourishment.

5
Indriya Nigraham (Senses)

5. Mei / Touch

Mastery over physical impulses and sensory attachments.

6
Arishadvarga (Internal Enemies)

6. Kama / Lust

Overcoming lustful desires and material obsessions.

7
Arishadvarga (Internal Enemies)

7. Krodha / Anger

Conquering wrath and establishing calm serenity in thoughts.

8
Arishadvarga (Internal Enemies)

8. Lobha / Greed

Eradicating avarice through generous charitable giving and Annadhaanam.

9
Arishadvarga (Internal Enemies)

9. Moha / Delusion

Transcending illusions of the transient worldly realm.

10
Arishadvarga (Internal Enemies)

10. Madha / Pride

Dissolving ego, status, and arrogance before the Divine Lord.

11
Arishadvarga (Internal Enemies)

11. Matsarya / Envy

Rejoicing in the joy of others and practicing universal fellowship.

12
Tri Guna (Inner Attributes)

12. Satva Guna / Purity

Cultivating goodness, wisdom, peace, and spiritual illumination.

13
Tri Guna (Inner Attributes)

13. Rajo Guna / Passion

Channeling restless energy towards devotional service and selflessness.

14
Tri Guna (Inner Attributes)

14. Tamo Guna / Inertia

Dispelling darkness, laziness, ignorance, and lethargy.

15
Punarjanma & Vidya

15. Vidya / Divine Knowledge

Attaining scriptural wisdom and supreme spiritual clarity.

16
Punarjanma & Vidya

16. Avidya / Transcending Ignorance

Breaking the veil of ego and ignorance bounding the soul.

17
Supreme Wisdom

17. Jnana / Self Realization

Realizing the oneness of the Jivatma with the Paramatma.

18
Ultimate Goal

18. Moksha / Mukthi

Final liberation and permanent union with Lord Manikanta (Tat Tvam Asi).

Sacred Hymn

Harivarasanam — The Divine Lullaby

Composed by Sri Kambangudi Kulathu Iyer, this sublime Sanskrit hymn is sung every night at Sabarimala before closing the sanctum.

Verse 1
Harivarasanam Viswamohanam
Haridadhiswaram Aaradhyapadhukam
Arivimardhanam Nithyanarthanam
Hariharathmajam Devamashraye || 1 ||
Meaning: I take refuge in Lord Hariharatmaja, who is the abode of blessings (Harivarasanam), who enchants the universe, who is worshipped by Indra and gods, who destroys enemies (inner foes), and who dances in eternal bliss.
Verse 2
Saranakeerthanam Bakhtamanasam
Bharanalolupam Narthanalasam
Arunabhasuram Bhoothanayakam
Hariharathmajam Devamashraye || 2 ||
Meaning: I take refuge in Lord Hariharatmaja, who is pleased with songs of refuge (Saranam chants), who dwells in the hearts of devotees, who sustains the cosmos, radiant like the rising sun, and who is the Lord of all beings.
Chorus / Pallavi
Saranam Ayyappa Swami Saranam Ayyappa
Saranam Ayyappa Swami Saranam Ayyappa ||
Refuge in You, Lord Ayyappa, ultimate refuge in You.
Pilgrim Guidelines

Sacred Vrutham & Irumudi Kettu Vidhi

Guidelines for observing the mandatory 41-day Mandala Vrutham and preparing the sacred two-compartment Irumudi bag.

Mala Dharanam & Deeksha

Wearing the sacred Thulasi or Rudraksha garland from the Guruswami marks the beginning of the 41-day strict austerities, celibacy, and daily pooja.

Neyyabhishekam Ghee Coconut

Filling pure cow ghee into the cleaned coconut through the third eye symbolizes offering one's purified soul (Atma) directly to Lord Ayyappa.

18 Padi Climb & Darshanam

Ascending the holy Pathinettampadi carrying the sacred Irumudi on head, culminating in the transcendent vision of the Lord and Makaravilakku Jyothi.
