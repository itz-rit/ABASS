Charitable Objects of the Trust

The objects for which the Trust is founded, as set out in the Trust Deed.

4. OBJECTS:

The objects for which the Trust is founded are:

(a)To establish, maintain and run the trust towards the religious - all kind of religious and temple activities and cultural related all.

(b)To establish any kind of educational institutions in the motive of giving Education.

(c)To maintain any temples and to involve in their activities,

(d)To Spread for promotion of religious ethics and moral values for the welfare of the society.

(e)To advance Indian Religious Culture and Literature, Service of this Country for the benefit of our Nation.

(f)To train teachers and workers in ideals and practice of the true spirit of the religious education.

(g)To establish centers for the furtherance trust activities in any fields and branches.

(h)To undertake propaganda, conducting training and education of the masses either of its own or in co-operation with similar Agencies working for the cause of all round development of the Society.

(i)To bring, publish and sell, distribute books, periodicals, leaflets, brochures and papers and also to open and maintain libraries, reading rooms for the promotion of the objects of the Trust.

(j)To function as a Non-communal Trust and as a secular organisation.

(k)To establish centres for employment generation.

(l)To acquire and maintain the movables and immovable properties for achieving and said objects.

(m)To advance any other objects of general public utility.

(n)To confer title on eminent personality in the field of science, education, literature, etc.,

(o)To relieve, the poor, the indigent persons, irrespective of caste, creed, religion, regional or nationality and make them to provide for their bare necessities of life.

(p)To construct, start, run, maintain and take over and develop homes, feeding houses for the aged orphans, destitute, women, working women, crèches.

(q)To arrange for relief operations to the affected in case of riots, natural calamities and drought and arrange for rehabilitation work.

(r)To promote encourage art, music, dance, architecture, fine arts and athletics of sports indoor and outdoor.

(s)To organize start, maintain lectures, seminars conferences, symposiums and workshops for the purpose of inculcating civic sense and health rules and spiritual uplift and also for imparting non-formal and adult education.

(t)To establish, maintain and run the Gurukulam/hostels for the benefit of students, children, women and any person who involved in the activities of the trust.

(u)To institute and award scholarships including loan scholarships without interest, and stipends and otherwise aid and assist study, research in apprenticeship for education and to award prizes to students for proficiency in education.

(v)To provide assistance in cash or in kind to poor and deserving students to prosecute studies in institutions imparting Higher educations.

(w)To open and run residential and non-residential educational institutions any part of India in accordance with the rules and regulations of the both State and Central Government, wherever such rules are prescribed.

(x)To help economically poor and deserving students to pursue higher education in the institutions run by the Trust and/or other agencies through scholarships and fellowships.

(y)To give socialistic, moral and employability oriented training to the students/unemployed graduates in the institutions run by the Trust and/or other agencies so that a sense of service to humanity is inculcated in them.

(z)To help economically poor and deserving students to pursue higher education in the institutions run by the Trust and/or other agencies through scholarships and fellowships.

(aa)To open and run schools for the all including blind, deaf, dumb, mentally retarded and other physically disabled persons.

(ab)To Set up and help by endowments, orphanages or poor houses for the benefits of orphans and poor persons and awarding of scholarships and cash payments to poor persons and widows desirous of receiving a primary, secondary and/or higher education and/or research in any branch or branches of science in its widest sense.

(ac)To pay tuition fees, examination fees, boarding and lodging fees to deserving students and to supply books free of cost to such students.

(ad)To make monthly or other periodical or lump-sum grants and contributions for the advancement of education of educational institutions.

(ae)To render financial help to deserving students to enable them to purchase books and other equipment’s necessary for their education.

(af)To organize entertainments like cinema, drama, music, concert, dance and other cultural performances to collect donations for the furtherance of the objects of the trust.

(ag)To undertake the conduct and management of educational and non-educational institutions belonging to other societies, or individuals, or Government, Central or State, for such periods and on such terms and conditions as the trustees may deem fit.

(ah)To construct buildings, and structures of whatever nature or kind as are found to be necessary or expedient and to demolish, alter, repair, renovate, improve, extend, develop the buildings or parts of them in furtherance of the objects of the Trust.

(ai)To purchase, to exchange, or to take on lease and licence or hire or otherwise deal with any movable or immovable property rights and privileges the trustees may deem necessary or desirable for the promotion or furtherance of any or all of the objects of the Trust.

(aj)To sell, exchange, alienate, lease, give out on lease or licence, mortgage, charge pledge, hypothecate, transfer, surrender, dispose off and/or otherwise deal with all or any of the property and assets of the Trust.

(ak)To apply for, receive, accept and utilize the Government, University and other grants, aids and other allowances from Non-Governmental aid agencies, associations or societies or corporations individuals in connection with any institutions, educational or otherwise, works and activities conducted and properties held and to deal with the same in accordance with the terms and conditions thereof to execute such documents therefore over all or any of the property of the Trust as may be required.

(al)To receive, accept, hold, expand, to charge and receive from students, trainees, boarders, such fees and charges as prescribed and normally received and administer and use and gift, bequest, grant, donation or foundation in kind or in money or any other property and raise moneys by fees and advertisements for the promotion of any of the objects of the Trust.

(am)To open and operate accounts of any description with any scheduled or nationalized banks or any other banks on such terms and in such manner as the trustees may deem fit.

(an)To employ teaching, non-teaching administrative and corporate governance staff in accordance with the rules of the Government wherever such rules are prescribed.

(ao)To borrow monies either on hand loans, on promissory notes, bonds, bills of exchange or on security of any of the properties of the Trust by deposit of title deeds by execution of registered documents, mortgage, charge, security of hypothecation as the trustees may think fit, so as to enable the Trust to carry on the objects of the Trust effectively.

(ap)To fix the name of the educational institutions to be established by the Trust as the Trustees may deem fit.

(aq)The Trust shall have the rights to authorise the educational institutions established under its auspices to enter into MOUS, affiliations and obtain franchise to offer their accredited programmes

(ar)The Trust shall have the rights to enter into Partnership, Joint-venture and Association with official Association and federations to launch educational programmes, conduct national and international conferences and publish periodicals and other publications.

(as)The Trust shall have the rights to offer training programmes, both technical and Managerial, techno managerial consultancy for the higher education institutions, industries and business establishments.
