Annadhaanam Mahadhanam

Provide wholesome prasadam and meals to over 250+ pilgrims and destitute individuals during monthly poojas and Sastha Preethi festivals.

  • Sankalpam performed in your family's name & gothram
  • Special Prasadam packet couriered / handed over
  • Audit receipt & acknowledgement certificate

18 Padi Pooja Sponsorship

Sponsor the complete 18 sacred step floral decoration, 18 kalasam lighting, ghee lamps, and special Sastha Sahasranama archana.

  • Exclusive front-row seating during Deepa Aradhana
  • Special 18 Padi prasadam & blessed silk vastram
  • Name displayed on the festival sponsor honor board
Sponsor Padi Pooja

Thulasi & Pushpalankaram

Sponsor sacred Thulasi garlands, fragrant lotus flowers, vana mala, and sandalwood paste for Lord Ayyappa's daily and festival alankaram.

  • Garland offered on the chosen birth star / anniversary
  • Sacred Chandana & Vibhoothi Prasadam
  • Digital photo of the decorated deity
Sponsor Alankaram

Nithya Deepam & Pooja Ghee

Provide pure cow ghee, sesame oil, and organic camphor for the unextinguished Nithya Deepam at the sanctum sanctorum.

  • Perpetual prayer blessing for family well-being
  • Archana performed with your family sankalpam
  • Tax audit receipt with reference number

Fund emergency medical camps, free glucose/hydration stalls, foot care, and first-aid support for walking pilgrims during Mandala season.

  • Direct impact on hundreds of weary pilgrims
  • Volunteer & donor recognition certificate
  • Detailed medical camp service summary report

Transparent Process

A Simple, Transparent Giving Journey

Every contribution is recorded with a receipt, a donation reference and full reconciliation support.

1

Choose Pooja / Event / Cause

Select from active poojas, festival sponsorships, monthly Annadhaanam, or social welfare causes.

2

Select a Sponsorship or Cause

Tell the Trustees what you wish to support; they will confirm the details and the official bank / UPI information with you.

3

Provide Donor Details

Name, Gothram, Star, Rasi, and mobile number are recorded for divine pooja sankalpam and archana.

4

Secure Bank Transfer / UPI Payment

Transfer directly to the official ABASS Trust bank account with verified IFSC and UPI handle.

5

Official Receipt & Prasadam Confirmation

Receive an instant digital receipt with a unique donation reference number for your accounting records.
